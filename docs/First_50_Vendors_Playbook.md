# First 50 Vendors Playbook — ELBOLD Events

**Version:** 1.0  
**Date:** June 2026  
**Owner:** Founder  
**Goal:** 50 approved, active vendors across Essex, Kent, and London within 30 days of outreach start

---

## Why the First 50 Matter

The first 50 vendors are not just the beginning of the marketplace — they are the marketplace. Customers who visit ELBOLD in the first 90 days will see these vendors. Their profile quality, their responsiveness, and their professionalism will determine whether ELBOLD earns a second visit.

A sparse vendor list destroys credibility immediately. A targeted, curated list of 50 verified professionals — even in just three counties — creates a platform that feels real, trusted, and worth booking from.

**The job is not to get 50 vendors. The job is to get 50 vendors who will make every customer experience excellent.**

## Recruitment Sources

### 1. Instagram / TikTok (Highest Volume)

**How:**
- Search hashtags: #EssexDJ #EssexPhotographer #EssexDecorator #LondonEventVendor etc.
- Look for accounts with:
  - 500–10,000 followers (micro-vendors, more likely to join early)
  - Active posting (last post within 14 days)
  - Bookings visible in their content (real events, not just studio shots)
  - Caption mentions "enquiries" or "bookings open"
- DM template (see Outreach Templates below)

**Volume to aim for:** 10 DMs per day = 70 per week. Expect 10–15% positive response.

### 2. Facebook Groups

**Relevant groups:**
- "Essex Events & Entertainment"
- "UK Wedding Suppliers & Vendors"
- "London DJ Community"
- "Essex Wedding Photographers Group"
- "Freelance Event Professionals UK"
- Local "Buy and Sell / Services" groups in each county

**How:**
- Join groups as ELBOLD (or founder personal profile)
- Post introduction: "We're building a verified marketplace for event professionals — free to list, Stripe-secured bookings — looking for founding vendors in Essex/Kent/London"
- DM vendors who post their services in groups

### 3. Google Search

**Search terms:**
- "DJ hire Essex"
- "photographer Kent wedding"
- "event decorator London prices"
- "[category] hire [city]" for every category + target city

**How:**
- Find vendors without strong online presence (no proper website or Bark/Poptop listing)
- Contact via website contact form or email
- These vendors are likely to be most interested — they need more reach

### 4. Existing Marketplaces (Counter-positioning)

**Bark, Poptop, Hitched, Bark.com, Add to Event:**
- Search for vendors in target categories and locations
- Many vendors are frustrated with subscription fees and low-quality leads
- Approach with: "We're free to list, commission-only, and you own your profile"

**How:**
- Find the vendor's personal website or social media from their marketplace profile
- Contact directly, not through the other marketplace
- Lead with the comparison: "Unlike Bark, you don't pay to receive leads"

### 5. Personal Referrals

**Ask every vendor you approve:** "Do you know 2 other vendors in a different category who would be a good fit for ELBOLD?"

A referred vendor from a trusted peer is easier to approve, more likely to maintain standards, and more likely to stay active.

**Track referral sources** — if one vendor category or region is high-referral, double down there.

### 6. Event Industry Groups & Associations

- UK Alliance of Wedding Planners
- National Association of DJs
- British Institute of Professional Photography (BIPP)
- Events Industry Alliance UK
- Regional wedding expos and business networking events

Attend or exhibit at one local event expo in Essex or London in months 1–2.

## Outreach Process

### Step 1 — Research (5–10 minutes per vendor)

Before contacting, verify:
- [ ] Active social presence (posted in last 30 days)
- [ ] Real work visible (not stock photos)
- [ ] Located in target area
- [ ] Category matches ELBOLD's offering
- [ ] No obvious red flags (bad reviews visible, fake follower count, etc.)

If they pass: add to outreach tracker (spreadsheet or CRM).

### Step 2 — First Contact

**Channel:** Instagram DM or Facebook message (most likely to be seen)

**Template — Instagram DM:**
```
Hi [Name], 

I came across your work and it's genuinely impressive. 

I'm building ELBOLD — a new marketplace connecting event professionals with customers across Essex, Kent and London. It's free to list, and we only earn a small commission when a booking completes (no monthly fees).

We're inviting a small number of founding vendors. Benefits:
- Permanent top placement in search results
- Founding Vendor badge on your profile
- Stripe-secured payments on every booking
- Free for as long as you use it

If you're interested, I can send a link to apply. Takes 5 minutes.

[Your name] — ELBOLD
```

Keep it short. Do not paste the whole benefits list. The goal of the first message is just a reply.

### Step 3 — Follow-Up (48 hours after no response)

```
Hi [Name] — just following up on my message about ELBOLD. Happy to answer any questions if you have them. The founding spots are limited to 20 vendors and are going quickly.
```

One follow-up only. If no response to follow-up, mark as "No Response" and move on.

### Step 4 — Interested

Once vendor replies positively:
1. Send the apply link: `https://www.elbold.com/vendor/apply`
2. Answer any questions about the process, fees, verification
3. Offer to jump on a 10-minute call if they hesitate

### Step 5 — Applied

Once application arrives:
1. Review within 24 hours (this is the brand promise — keep it)
2. If approved: send personalised welcome message (in addition to the automated email)
3. If rejected: be honest and specific — "We don't yet have enough customer demand in [area]" or "We're looking for vendors with an active portfolio"

### Step 6 — Approved → Active

Goal: move every approved vendor from approved to "active" (complete profile + first enquiry) within 7 days.

**Activation checklist to send every new vendor:**
- [ ] Upload at least 5 portfolio images
- [ ] Set your pricing / packages
- [ ] Set your availability calendar
- [ ] Add your bio (at least 100 words)
- [ ] Share your profile link on your social media

**Follow up personally** with every vendor who has been approved but hasn't completed their profile within 5 days.

## Quality Over Quantity

**Do not approve vendors who:**
- Have no real portfolio (no photos of actual work)
- Have suspicious or stock-photo-only profiles
- Are not located in the target areas
- Have consistently bad reviews on other platforms
- Cannot be identified as a real person running a real service

**The 20 founding vendor slots exist because we want 20 excellent vendors, not 20 warm bodies.**

A marketplace with 20 great vendors and 100 customer enquiries is better than a marketplace with 50 mediocre vendors and 10 bookings.

## Messaging Principles

When talking to vendors about ELBOLD, lead with these truths — they are more persuasive than any feature list:

1. **"You only pay when you get paid."** No monthly fees. Commission only on completed bookings.
2. **"Customers searching ELBOLD are ready to book."** Not social media followers. People with a real event and a date.
3. **"Your profile is yours forever."** No algorithm. Permanent placement based on founding status.
4. **"We verify our vendors."** This means customers trust the platform — which means enquiries are higher quality than cold DMs.
