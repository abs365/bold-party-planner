## Structured Feedback Prompt

Send this to each tester after they complete their first full journey:

```
Hi [Name],

Thank you for testing ELBOLD. Could you answer these 5 questions? Takes 3 minutes.

1. What worked well?
2. What was confusing or frustrating?
3. Was there anything you expected to find that wasn't there?
4. Did anything break or not work at all?
5. Overall score out of 10 — and what would make it a 10?

Reply here or on WhatsApp. Your feedback is shaping the platform directly.
```
