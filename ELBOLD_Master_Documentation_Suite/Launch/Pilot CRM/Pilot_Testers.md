## Status Definitions

| Status | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `Invited` | Invitation sent, not yet signed up |
| `Signed Up` | Created account, not yet confirmed email |
| `Confirmed` | Email confirmed, logged in at least once |
| `Active` | Completed at least one journey step beyond login |
| `Stalled` | Has not engaged in 48+ hours after confirmation |
| `Complete` | Has completed the target journey end-to-end |
| `Dropped` | Left pilot — note reason |
